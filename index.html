Main project

The one I'm proudest of — because it almost didn't work.

🛗 Ghost-Free Elevator

An Arduino elevator that skips floors where the button was pressed but nobody is actually waiting.

The problem

On busy mornings, the elevator in my building stopped at floor after floor with no one there. My dad joked that a ghost must be riding it.

My idea

A motion sensor on each floor checks if a real person is waiting. Blue light: someone is there, stop. Red light: empty, pass.

What was hard

I had never coded before. I bought an Arduino kit and struggled just to understand it. It only worked after a teacher showed me how the board works and how to wire it.

What I learned

Having an idea is the easy part. Making it work takes patience, help, and a lot of starting over.

  1. 2023 · 6th gradev1 — a concept model. Put a doll on a floor and the light turns on: someone is waiting. Entered in the district student science & invention fair.
  2. Apr 2024v2 — learned Arduino, then coded and wired a working version. Silver prize at the same fair.
  3. May 2024Gold prize (1st of 9 teams), school invention contest.
